Admin View

Help for the admin form.

Usage

The admin form presents you a slew of managment actions which you can interact with by pressing the button of what you want to do.

You can view what each one does by clicking on them in the pages list of the help menu, or you may click the help button in their respective forms.

You may also log out by pressing the button in the top right.
