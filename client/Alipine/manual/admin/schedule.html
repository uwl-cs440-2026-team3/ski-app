Race Rescheduling

Help for the race scheduling form.

Usage

This form allows you to schedule a new race.

Races have some limitations:

Races must be in the future.

New races can not lead to team time conflicts (EG: if you have a race scheduled at 1:30 between team Fast and team Speed you cannot also schedule a race between team Fast and team Quick at 1:30).

New races can not lead to course time conflicts (EG: if you have a race scheduled at 1:30 on course Wet Hill you cannot then schedula another race on wet hill at that same time).

The above conflicts have a period of 30 minutes around them where they will still count as a conflict (EG: scheduling a new race at 1:20 or 1:40 would also cause a conflict).
