Login and Registration

Help for the registration and log in form.

Usage

On this form you can register your skier account or log in to your account.

Registration requires you to fill out all three forms while cohering to their form requirements: An email must follow the local@domain format, a password must be more then 8 characters long, fields may not be absurdly long.

Login only requires you to provide your email and password.
